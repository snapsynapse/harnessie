Package and assistant guide both use 1.5.0. The exact receipts are in [audits/release-1.5.0.md](audits/release-1.5.0.md).

Harnessie 1.5.0 makes a Harnessie result identifiable and reconcilable from outside the harness. Every verify report, run report and event log names the harness that produced it. The registry refusals a brain triggers by speaking another harness's tool vocabulary are counted as tool-contract breaks and published per brain. A run's hash-chained event log exports as an ATIF-v1.7 trajectory that an independent capture can be checked against. Two examples put Harnessie on either side of a Harbor trial, as the verifier and as the external agent, without changes to either project.

## Included

- Harness identity on every result (`harness/identity.py`). `harnessie verify` reports and `harnessie report` print the installed harness version, the inward-manifest digest (or `absent`) and a digest of the registered tool surface beside the brain's bundle identity. The standalone verifier and the workflow runner emit the same fields as one `harness_identity` event, and the ATIF exporter carries them under `agent.extra`.
- Tool-contract break metrics. `harness/trace_eval.py` adds `refusals_by_error`, `tool_contract_breaks` (refusals with `action_unsupported`, `malformed_arguments` or `bad_arguments`), `completed_tasks` and `tool_calls_per_completed_task` (`null` when no loop completed). Policy refusals such as consent, role and approval are not counted. Loop eval scenarios accept `expect_trace`, and `evals/tool-contract.yaml` proves one foreign tool call is recoverable, a repeated one ends `stuck`, malformed arguments count, and a consent refusal does not.
- The `tool_contract` live scorecard row and the first measured brains. [docs/brains.md](docs/brains.md) gains the per-brain table, filled from a Sam-approved local-only run on 2026-10-05 recorded in [audits/live-scorecard-2026-10-05/](audits/live-scorecard-2026-10-05/README.md): `qwen3.8:latest` and `gpt-oss:20b` completed with zero contract breaks, `qwen3.8:latest` regressed under placeholder substitution, and `granite4.1-guardian:8b` made no tool calls so its break count is not measurable.
- `harnessie atif RUN_ID_OR_DIR [--out PATH] [--force]` exports a run's or verify report's event log as an ATIF-v1.7 `trajectory.json`, Harbor's trajectory format. It makes no model calls and refuses a broken hash chain, a loop without `loop_finished`, a step gap, or an existing output without `--force`. Fields the log does not hold are reported as absent. To make the record reconcilable, `model_turn` events now also carry `input_tokens`, `output_tokens`, `tool_call_ids`, `model`, `provider` and `effort`, and `tool_result` events carry `call_id`; the existing `tokens` total is unchanged. The [export acceptance](audits/atif-export-acceptance-2026-10-05.md) records a Harbor-valid trajectory whose call count matched OpenEnv's capture proxy.
- `examples/harbor-verifier/`: Harnessie as a Harbor verifier in both shapes Harbor supports. `harnessie_verifier.py` is the host-side `BaseVerifier` that runs `harnessie verify --no-verifier` under Harnessie's own OS sandbox; `tasks/calc-add-mean/tests/test.sh` is the in-sandbox form. Exit 0 maps to reward 1, exit 1 to reward 0, and exit 2 to no reward file. Only deterministic checks reach the reward. The [verifier acceptance](audits/harbor-verifier-acceptance-2026-10-05.md) records four Harbor 0.24.0 trials on the Docker backend.
- `examples/harbor-agent/`: Harnessie as a Harbor external agent. `harbor_tools.py` registers `read_file`, `list_files`, `write_file`, `run_shell` and `task_complete` on an ordinary `ToolRegistry` with the builtin worker policy unchanged, with effects landing in the Harbor environment; `harnessie_harbor_agent.py` is the `BaseAgent` wrapper that writes `events.jsonl`, an ATIF `trajectory.json` and `harnessie-result.json` into the trial and turns Harbor's agent timeout into a recorded `model_error` stop through `CancellableModel`. The [agent acceptance](audits/harbor-agent-acceptance-2026-10-05.md) records three trials, one with Harnessie as both agent and verifier. The `calc-add-mean` task's agent timeout is now 1,200 s.

## Authority and evidence limits

The ATIF export is derived from the audit stream and invents nothing. Model messages and system prompts are absent (`message` is empty with `extra.message_recorded: false`), tool arguments are absent (`arguments_recorded: false`), and tool output is the 300-character excerpt the log keeps. The exporter reports those gaps rather than reconstructing them. A schema-valid trajectory whose call count matches a capture is evidence that the harness's record and the wire agree on structure; it is not evidence that the model behaved well, and it is not token-level agreement.

The harness identity names the installed version, the inward-manifest digest and a digest over the registered tool surface. These are reported identities. They do not authenticate who ran the harness, prove the source was unmodified beyond what the inward manifest covers, or establish that a result was produced independently. The tool set digest covers names, schemas, effects and grants, not implementations, so the remote Harbor tool surface and the builtin registry have different digests under the same policy.

Tool-contract counts are per bundle: one brain, one endpoint, one prompt hash, one parser version, one sampling setting, one short tool-using task. The only measured brains so far ran on a local Ollama endpoint; no cloud provider was reachable in that run and both cloud targets report `SKIP`. A brain that makes no tool calls has no measurable break count, and a safety classifier's passes on no-action rows are not evidence about the small-model floor. Any change to model, endpoint, prompts, parser or sampling requires a new run; the numbers do not carry over.

The Harbor examples are contract and plumbing results on one backend (Docker through Colima), two tasks, and one local model. They establish that Harnessie's verdict contract maps onto Harbor's reward contract and that Harnessie's policy enforcement survives the move to a remote sandbox. They are not evidence about any brain's quality. The in-sandbox verifier form failed closed because the container runtime denies bubblewrap its namespaces; that is the harness working as specified, not a scoring result. The host-side agent runs with the operator's authority, as any Harbor external agent does; Harnessie's own OS sandbox is not engaged for it because nothing executes on the host.

Cannot-verify is unscorable, never zero. A `harnessie verify` exit 2 produces no reward file. Harbor carries that outcome two ways, a completed trial with no score from a custom verifier or an errored trial from a missing reward file, and neither is a zero. Writing a zero would teach a policy that an unverifiable result is a failure to avoid. Verifier model verdicts are never used as a training signal in these examples.

The 1.5.0 pin, proposing Verify Action 0.2.3, is in progress at https://github.com/snapsynapse/harnessie-verify-action/pull/4 with all seven fixtures green; it is not merged or released. Homebrew 1.4.1 is published at tap merge `953760f3968200f99658bfb068609532cb9fbf4d`; the 1.5.0 Homebrew 1.5.0 formula is in progress at https://github.com/snapsynapse/homebrew-tap/pull/5, where the exact formula passed `brew audit --strict --online`, a real 1.4.1 to 1.5.0 upgrade, `brew test` and `brew linkage --test`; it is not merged. Both are separately propagated downstreams and continue to serve 1.4.1 until their own releases land; pinning follows. Engine wrappers remain on their independent 0.1.0 release train.

Token-level ATIF agreement, the full validated-harness bar OpenEnv applies, needs the capture proxy in front of a token-returning engine such as vLLM or SGLang. Local Ollama yields evaluation-only rollouts, which is enough for call-count agreement and for every other shipped candidate. That remains a GPU-host decision.

The in-sandbox Harbor verifier form could score if the task image ran with the capabilities bubblewrap needs, or if Harnessie gained a backend that confines without namespaces. Neither was attempted. Record-mode tool aliases, the containment note for proxied endpoints and the training-surface scope note remain proposed candidates in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md); the two marked contested need human-arbitrated decisions.

The controlled-review pilot's local Qwen queue continues as recorded in [NEXT.md](NEXT.md); its consumed attempts and approvals are unchanged by this release. Human accessibility acceptance remains a deferred nonblocking roadmap item; no full-conformance claim is made. Live review panels, bidding, commentary, follow mode, automatic runner observation and bid-driven model selection remain an evidence-gated capability sequence.
